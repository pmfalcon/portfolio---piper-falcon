Since 1976

Our Story

Formed out of a chance partnership between engineer Alan Parsons and songwriter Eric Woolfson, the band set out to make records that worked as complete artistic statements rather than collections of singles.

What started as a studio experiment became a run of concept albums, each built around a single idea and carried through with cinematic arrangements, guest vocalists, and meticulous production. That approach hasn't changed — only the tools have.

We don't write songs. We write sides.
